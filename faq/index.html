Signing in and your account

How do I sign in? Is there a password?

There are no passwords. Enter your email address and we'll email you a 6-digit code. Type it in to sign in. The code works for 10 minutes and you get 5 tries. If you're new, you'll then pick a username and display name, enter your birthdate, and agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy.

I didn't get my sign-in code. What should I do?

Check your spam or junk folder first, and make sure you typed your email address correctly. If it still isn't there, wait 30 seconds and tap to resend; a new code replaces the old one. If codes never arrive, email team@rekap.app.

How old do I have to be to use REKAP?

You must be at least 13. If a birthdate under 13 is entered at sign-up, sign-up stops and the account that was just created, along with its email address, is deleted. Tips and REKAP Live are for people 18 and older only. If your account was made before we asked for birthdays, we'll ask once the first time you try to tip or use Live; if it shows you're under 13, the account is deleted.

How do I edit my profile?

Go to your profile and edit it to change your display name, profile photo, bio, profile link, and favourite sports and teams. Remember that your profile is public.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account and type to confirm. It takes effect immediately and signs you out everywhere: your REKAPs, comments, likes, follows, Live bookings and profile details (email, username, photo, birthdate and more) are deleted. Messages you already sent stay in the other person's Inbox and tip records are kept for our financial records, both shown as "Deleted account" with no link to you. Backups are cleared within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for the full list.

Safety and reporting

How do I report a REKAP?

On any REKAP that isn't yours, tap "…" then Report, and pick a reason: Spam, Harassment or bullying, Hate, Sexual content, Violence or dangerous acts, Copyright / I own this content, or Something else. We review reports, act within 24 hours where possible, and may remove content or suspend accounts.

How do I block someone?

Open the person's profile and choose Block. Blocked people can't message you, and you won't show up in their Send or search suggestions.

Someone posted my video. How do I get it taken down?

Use Report → "Copyright / I own this content" on that REKAP, or email team@rekap.app with your contact info, a description of your work, the REKAP link, a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised, a statement that your info is accurate and you're the owner or authorised to act for them, and your signature. Repeat infringers lose their accounts.

Features

What are tips and REKAP Live?

Tips let you send a voluntary gift to a creator, and REKAP Live lets you book or host a live show. Both are 18+ only. Tips aren't live yet: no real payments are processed right now.

How do I share a REKAP?

Use Send to share a REKAP with someone in your Inbox, or copy its link. Shared /r/ links let anyone watch that one REKAP, even if they aren't signed in.

Can I post clips from a TV broadcast?

No. Only post content you own or have the rights to. TV or broadcast footage, league footage, and other people's clips aren't allowed unless you have permission. See our Terms.

Contact

How do I contact REKAP, and how fast will you reply?

Email team@rekap.app. We reply within 2 business days. For urgent safety issues, use Report in the app right away. Visit our Support page for more.
