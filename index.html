FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I sign in? Is there a password?

There are no passwords. Enter your email address and we'll email you a 6-digit code. Type it in to sign in. The code works for 10 minutes and you get 5 tries. If you're new, you'll then pick a username and display name, enter your birthdate, and agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy.

How old do I have to be to use REKAP?

You must be at least 13. If a birthdate under 13 is entered at sign-up, sign-up stops and the account that was just created, along with its email address, is deleted. Booking or hosting a REKAP Live show is for people 18 and older only. If your account was made before we asked for birthdays, we'll ask once the first time you try to use Live; if it shows you're under 13, the account is deleted.

How do I report a REKAP?

On any REKAP that isn't yours, tap "…" then Report, and pick a reason: Spam, Harassment or bullying, Hate, Sexual content, Violence or dangerous acts, Copyright / I own this content, or Something else. We review reports, act within 24 hours where possible, and may remove content or suspend accounts.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account and type to confirm. It takes effect immediately and signs you out everywhere: your REKAPs, comments, likes, follows, Live bookings and profile details (email, username, photo, birthdate and more) are deleted. Messages you already sent stay in the other person's Inbox, shown as "Deleted account" with no link to you, and the few records we must keep are listed in the Privacy Policy. Backups are cleared within 30 days.
