Who we are

REKAP is a short-video sports app operated by Hotake, LLC ("Hotake", "we", "us"). You can reach us at team@rekap.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use REKAP on the web or in our apps, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

This policy works together with our Terms of Service. If you have a question that this page doesn't answer, email us at team@rekap.app.

What we collect

Information you give us.

  • Sign-in details: your email address. REKAP doesn't use passwords. Each time you sign in, we email you a 6-digit code.
  • Account details: your username, display name and birthdate, and a record that you agreed to our Terms and this Privacy Policy.
  • Profile details you choose to add: a profile photo, bio, a profile link, and your favourite sports and teams.
  • Content you post: your videos (we call them REKAPs), along with their titles and captions, and the comments you write.
  • Messages: the direct messages you send and receive in your Inbox.
  • Reports and blocks: the reports you submit (including the reason you choose) and the people you block.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you email support.

Information created when you use REKAP.

  • Activity: likes, follows, saves and shares.
  • Tips and REKAP Live: tips you send or receive and live shows you book or host. Tips are currently switched off or in test mode only, and no real payments are processed yet. If we turn on real payments, we'll update this policy first to explain what payment information is collected and by whom.
  • Device and log information: your IP address, browser and device type, and server logs that record requests to our service (for example, the time of a request and whether it succeeded or failed).

We don't ask for your phone number, contacts, precise location or government ID.

How we use your information

  • To run your account: sending sign-in codes, keeping you signed in, and showing your profile.
  • To provide the app: storing, processing and playing your REKAPs (including making different video sizes and thumbnails), showing comments, delivering messages, and powering likes, follows, saves and shares.
  • To personalise what you see: for example, using the sports and teams you follow and the accounts you follow to decide which REKAPs to show you. We don't use your information to target ads.
  • To enforce age limits: we use your birthdate to confirm you are at least 13, and that you are 18 or older before you can use tips or REKAP Live.
  • To keep REKAP safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our rules, suspending accounts, applying blocks, and detecting spam, abuse and security threats.
  • To communicate with you: answering support requests and telling you about important changes to the service, our Terms or this policy.
  • To fix and improve REKAP: using logs to find bugs, measure performance and understand how features are used.
  • To meet legal obligations: responding to valid legal requests and reporting child sexual exploitation material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by law.

What is public

REKAP is a public sharing app. By default, the following can be seen by anyone using REKAP:

  • Your profile: username, display name, profile photo, bio, profile link, and favourite sports and teams.
  • Your REKAPs, with their titles and captions.
  • Your comments.
  • Like and follower counts.

When you or someone else shares a REKAP using its /r/ link, people who aren't signed in can watch that one REKAP. Direct messages are only visible to the people in that chat. Please don't post anything in your profile, REKAPs or comments that you don't want the public to see.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising (as those terms are used in US state privacy laws). We share information only in these situations:

  • Service providers: companies that help us run REKAP, such as Amazon Web Services, which provides our hosting, database, video storage and processing, and sends our emails (including sign-in codes). They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Other users: the public information described above, and messages with the people you are chatting with.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when we believe in good faith that it's required by law or legal process, or needed to protect the safety of any person, to address fraud or abuse, or to protect the rights of Hotake, LLC and our users. This includes reports to NCMEC.
  • Business changes: if Hotake, LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that deal, and this policy will continue to apply to it.
  • With your permission: in any other case where you ask us to or agree.

Cookies

We keep cookies to a minimum. REKAP uses only:

  • a session cookie that keeps you signed in;
  • a CSRF cookie that protects forms from being submitted by other websites; and
  • one cookie that remembers you opened REKAP from our iOS app, so the site works correctly inside the app.

We don't use advertising cookies or third-party ad trackers. Because these cookies are needed for REKAP to work, blocking them in your browser may stop you from signing in.

How long we keep it and deleting your account

We keep your information for as long as your account is open, and for the limited periods described below after that.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account; you'll be asked to type to confirm. Deletion takes effect immediately and signs you out on every device.

What we delete:

  • your REKAPs, including the likes, comments and saves on them (their video and image files are removed from our storage within minutes);
  • your comments, likes, saves, follows and followers, and the blocks you made;
  • your REKAP Live bookings, Live chat messages and reminders;
  • your profile: email address, phone number, username, display name, photo, bio, link, birthdate and favourite sports and teams;
  • your sign-in codes, sign-in sessions, support requests and username history (links to your old usernames stop working).

What we keep, anonymized, and why: a few records involve other people or the law, so instead of deleting them we cut every link to you and show them as coming from "Deleted account":

  • messages you already sent stay in the other person's Inbox, because they are part of that person's conversation;
  • records of tips you sent or received, because we must keep financial records; and
  • reports you made about other content, so our moderation history stays complete.

These records point to an empty account entry with no name, email, phone, username, photo or birthdate. Your profile page stops working right away. Your old username is held for 14 days and then anyone can use it. You can sign up again with the same email at any time, as a new account.

Backups. Deleted data is removed from our active systems immediately and from our backups within 30 days.

If you can't access Settings, you can also ask us to delete your account by emailing team@rekap.app from the address on your account.

Deleting individual content. When you delete a single REKAP, it is removed from REKAP and its files are removed from storage shortly after. Copies other people already received in messages, or saved outside REKAP, are outside our control.

Children and age limits

You must be at least 13 years old to use REKAP. We ask for your birthdate when you create an account. If the birthdate entered shows you are under 13, sign-up is stopped and the account and email address that were just created are deleted. Accounts made before we asked for birthdays are asked once, the first time they try to tip or use REKAP Live; if that birthday is under 13, the account is deleted as described above.

Tips and REKAP Live (booking or hosting a live show) are only available to people who are 18 or older.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email team@rekap.app and we will delete it.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including in states such as California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon, you may have the right to:

  • Access and know: ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Delete: ask us to delete your personal information. You can also do this yourself with Settings → Delete account.
  • Correct: ask us to fix information that is inaccurate. You can edit most profile details yourself in the app.
  • Opt out: opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising, and certain profiling. We don't do any of these, so there is nothing to opt out of, but you're still welcome to send us a request.
  • Appeal: if we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision. We'll respond to the appeal in writing and, where your state law provides one, explain how to contact your state attorney general.
  • No discrimination: we won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

To make a request, email team@rekap.app. To protect your account, we may verify your identity by emailing the address on the account and asking you to confirm the request. You may use an authorised agent where your state law allows; we may ask the agent for proof of authority and still ask you to confirm directly.

We'll respond within the time required by applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), short-lived sign-in codes that expire after 10 minutes and allow only 5 tries, and access controls on our systems. No service can be perfectly secure, so please keep your email account secure: anyone who can read your email can sign in to your REKAP account.

Our servers are located in the United States. If you use REKAP from outside the US, your information will be processed in the US.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as REKAP changes, for example before real payments for tips are turned on. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. If we make a material change, we'll let you know in the app or by email before it takes effect.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: team@rekap.app.

All privacy requests (access, deletion, correction, opt-out or an appeal) and any other legal notices to Hotake, LLC go to that email address. We don't accept requests by post.
