Contact us

Need help with REKAP? Have a question about your account, a bug to report, or feedback to share? We're here to help.

Email: team@rekap.app

We reply within 2 business days.

Many common questions are answered in our FAQ.

Urgent safety issues

If you see something harmful, use Report in the app right away: tap "…" on the REKAP, then Report, and choose a reason. You can also block someone from their profile.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first (911 in the US).

Copyright requests

If a REKAP uses your work without permission, use Report → "Copyright / I own this content", or email team@rekap.app. Our Terms list what to include.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings → Delete account and type to confirm, or email us a deletion request. Our Privacy Policy explains what is deleted.
